## Risk Summary

The scenario creates five primary risks:

| Risk ID | Risk |
|---|---|
| **S1-R1** | Successful AI-assisted phishing compromises administrator credentials |
| **S1-R2** | Stolen credentials are used to authenticate as a privileged user |
| **S1-R3** | Excessive privileges expose sensitive patient information |
| **S1-R4** | Malicious authenticated activity is not detected quickly |
| **S1-R5** | PHI and PII are accessed or potentially exfiltrated |

These identifiers are specific to **Scenario 1** and are separate from the platform-wide STRIDE identifiers such as `S1`, `I1`, and `E1`.
